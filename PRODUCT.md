# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering collaborators, and creative partners visiting Emily Yu's personal portfolio to understand her software work, experience, and creative practice.

## Product Purpose

Present Emily first as a full-stack developer while making her photography practice discoverable as a distinct, intentional second facet of the same personal identity.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and personal. The experience should feel technically capable without becoming impersonal, and creative without obscuring the professional engineering story.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy portfolio effects, generic template styling, ornamental complexity, or transitions that make the photography work feel like an unrelated microsite.

## Design Principles

- Keep the programming portfolio as the clear default experience.
- Reveal photography as a deliberate second discipline, not a competing homepage.
- Let motion communicate the shift in medium and color without delaying navigation.
- Preserve the existing restrained typography, spacing, and interaction language.
- Make every transition usable with a keyboard and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain visible keyboard focus, semantic links and controls, sufficient contrast, and a reduced-motion path that navigates without the full-screen animation.
